# Handoff Contracts

## Handoff A — Gather → Synthesize
The Gather stage passes an **evidence packet** containing facts, definitions, source locations, examples, warnings, and gaps.

The next stage must not treat missing information as known information.

## Handoff B — Synthesize → Draft
The Synthesize stage passes a **topic outline** containing ordered concepts, relationships, prerequisites, comparisons, and evidence gaps.

The Draft stage turns that structure into readable notes without silently expanding unsupported claims.

## Handoff C — Draft → Review
The Draft stage passes the **candidate study notes** plus the earlier evidence/synthesis.

The Review stage checks the draft against the evidence before final formatting.

## Final handoff
The Review stage passes:
- final notes;
- flagged claims;
- source gaps;
- human verification list;
- final review status.
