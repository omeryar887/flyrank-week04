# Source-Grounded Study Notes Flow

```text
INPUT
  |
  v
[GATHER]
  |  Evidence packet
  v
[SYNTHESIZE]
  |  Structured topic outline
  v
[DRAFT]
  |  Candidate notes
  v
[REVIEW + FORMAT]
  |
  +--> Human verification flags
  |
  v
FINAL NOTES
```

## Core principle
Every stage receives a defined artifact from the previous stage. This prevents the workflow from becoming one large prompt where it is difficult to tell which part caused an error.
