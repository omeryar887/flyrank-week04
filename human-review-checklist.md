# Human Review Checklist

Before publishing or submitting notes, check:

- [ ] Every major claim is supported by the supplied source or clearly labeled as background/illustrative.
- [ ] No citation or source reference was invented.
- [ ] Technical terminology is used correctly.
- [ ] Important conditions and exceptions were not removed during simplification.
- [ ] Examples are technically sensible.
- [ ] Commands/code were verified if present.
- [ ] Conflicts or gaps are visible.
- [ ] The notes match the intended learner level.
- [ ] The final format is readable.
- [ ] The review status reflects the actual amount of checking performed.
